01 3PL / Contract Logistics

From zero data infrastructure to near real-time warehouse visibility

European contract logistics provider — data strategy engagement

Challenge

No data infrastructure at all: reporting was manual, fragmented across sites, and the operational data lived inside customer-owned WMS systems the company couldn't even access.

Approach

Negotiated direct access to customer Manhattan WMS databases, designed the data model and a streaming architecture on Snowflake and Kafka Confluent, and built interim automation so operations had usable numbers from week one.

Outcome

Near real-time visibility across warehouse operations, a KPI framework adopted by site management, and fully automated extraction workflows replacing manual reporting.

  • Snowflake
  • Kafka Confluent
  • Manhattan WMS
  • Power BI
  • Power Automate
02 Retail · Automotive · FMCG

Complex customer onboarding on Business Central & 3PL Dynamics

Multi-site 3PL onboarding portfolio — IT project management

Challenge

Demanding retail, automotive and FMCG customers each arriving with their own EDI standards, labeling requirements and SLAs — to be integrated into a shared Business Central / WMS environment without disrupting live operations.

Approach

Owned the full onboarding lifecycle: system configuration, EDI message flow design (EDIFACT, X12), process design with warehouse teams, UAT coordination, team preparation and go-live management.

Outcome

Five to ten complex onboarding projects delivered across multiple European sites, with stabilized EDI flows and warehouse teams ready on day one.

  • Dynamics 365 Business Central
  • Boltrics 3PL Dynamics
  • EDIFACT / X12
  • UAT
  • Go-live management
03 Automotive Tier-1

Crisis response and data-driven operations in automotive manufacturing

Tier-1 automotive supplier — crisis management & reporting automation

Challenge

The Suez Canal disruption put inbound material flows at risk of stopping production lines — while day-to-day decisions still relied on slow, manual reporting.

Approach

Coordinated material planners to secure premium freight on critical parts, then attacked the root problem: built automated Power BI dashboards and KPI frameworks to replace manual reporting across operations.

Outcome

No production line stoppages during the disruption, and a lasting shift to data-driven decision making once the crisis passed.

  • Crisis management
  • Material planning
  • Power BI
  • KPI design
  • Supplier coordination
